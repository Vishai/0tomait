## About

Most businesses do not need “an AI strategy.” They need to know which real process is
worth changing, what level of tool is sufficient, and who will own the result.

Through 0tomait, I examine one business area and separate five different answers people
often group together as AI automation:

- a better prompt;
- a feature already inside the software;
- ordinary automation;
- an existing product;
- an agent job.

An agent job is justified when work keeps arriving, context lives across systems,
decisions vary, actions need authority limits, and the real outcome can be verified later.
I install that operating loop: intake, state, tools, do / ask / never policy, evaluation,
recovery, deployment, and handover.
