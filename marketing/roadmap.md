# 90-day launch roadmap

## Days 0–7 — align the public surface

**Outcome:** everyone who clicks sees the same offer, proof, and next step.

- Deploy the AI Leverage Map and Agent Job Pilot site.
- Confirm the executable demo completes publicly.
- Add canonical, Open Graph, and social-preview assets.
- Enable Cloudflare Web Analytics.
- Add “How did you hear about 0tomait?” to the calendar booking form.
- Update Brandon's LinkedIn headline and About section.
- Select and approve the first twenty warm contacts.
- Publish the launch post and send the first five warm notes.

**Decision gate:** if people cannot explain the difference between a prompt and an agent
job after seeing the page, fix that explanation before adding more channels.

## Days 8–30 — earn five process walkthroughs

**Outcome:** buyer language and one paid engagement.

- Run the Last Real Item campaign in batches of five.
- Ask five people for one introduction each.
- Publish two AI-or-Not classifications per week.
- Share the Survival Audit once, then reuse it only in relevant conversations.
- Research ten Dropped Handoff accounts per week; send only after a real signal is found.
- Hold process walkthroughs around actual examples.
- Quote a Map when uncertainty is the problem; quote a Pilot when the recurring job,
  value, authority, and completion test are already clear.

**Decision gate:** after ten walkthroughs, choose one workflow cluster only if at least
three buyers describe substantially the same problem in their own words.

## Days 31–60 — narrow and compound

**Outcome:** one repeatable message for one visible workflow family.

- Turn the strongest walkthrough pattern into a dedicated one-page teardown.
- Publish one approved case note from a paid engagement when evidence exists.
- Connect Apollo and use the proven workflow language to find thirty matching accounts.
- Start the LinkedIn newsletter only if the short posts have already produced replies.
- Approach ten operator partners with the now-specific use case.
- Create one small live process teardown session with a partner or operator group.

**Decision gate:** targeted outbound scales only if the first thirty accounts produce at
least two substantive replies or one walkthrough. If not, change the audience/signal pair,
not the email adjectives.

## Days 61–90 — build the durable channel

**Outcome:** one to two pilot starts per month without exhausting the network.

- Publish the first approved before/after case study.
- Create three high-intent pages based on real buyer phrasing, not generic AI keywords.
- Ask every satisfied client and active partner for one precise introduction.
- Record delivery hours, approval load, model cost, support time, and change requests.
- Review the $3,500 scope after three paid pilots.
- Decide whether HubSpot is justified by follow-up volume.
- Consider paid promotion only for a page and message that already convert direct traffic.

## SEO role

SEO is necessary as credibility and compounding infrastructure, but it is not the engine
for the first client.

Do now:

- canonical URLs, titles, descriptions, Open Graph images, sitemap, robots, and structured
  service data;
- Search Console registration and sitemap submission;
- clear internal links among the homepage, demo, audit, and method;
- fast static pages and accessible headings.

Do after buyer conversations:

- pages for the exact recurring jobs buyers name, such as contract-to-kickoff handoff,
  quote-intake reconciliation, or onboarding exception handling;
- case studies with an approved outcome, systems involved, authority boundary, and proof;
- comparisons that answer a real buying question: ChatGPT vs automation vs agent job.

Do not do yet:

- dozens of generic “AI for small business” posts;
- paid backlinks, synthetic location pages, or unreviewed model-generated articles;
- optimizing for traffic without a qualified-call and revenue measure.

## Capacity plan

The first target is one pilot per month. Move to two after delivery evidence shows that
two concurrent builds can maintain quality. Reaching four clients per month should mean a
mix of Maps, Pilots, and Run-and-Improve clients unless implementation capacity expands.
