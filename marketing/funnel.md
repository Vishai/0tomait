# How campaigns become closed work

Traffic is not the sales system. Every campaign must move a buyer through the same small
set of evidence gates.

## Funnel

| Stage | The buyer has done | 0tomait must learn | Exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reached | Seen a note, post, introduction, demo, or audit | Is the audience and signal relevant? | A reply, useful comment, referral, or site visit |
| Engaged | Described a process or asked a buying question | Is there a last real item? | Process walkthrough booked |
| Walkthrough | Shown one actual example end to end | Trigger, systems, judgment, action, approval, proof, frequency, failure cost | Classified opportunity |
| Qualified | Established recurring value and an accountable owner | Is scope clear enough to build? | Map or Pilot recommended |
| Map | Paid for discovery where uncertainty remains | Ranked opportunities, strongest acceptance test, build quote | Pilot proposal or simpler-tool plan |
| Proposal | Agreed on job, boundary, acceptance, dates, and owner | Does the buyer accept scope, price, and payment terms? | Signed and first 50% paid |
| Won | Started paid work | Can delivery create approved proof and an introduction? | Handover, case permission, referral ask |

## Qualification rule

An Agent Job Pilot is qualified only when most of these are true:

- New items enter the process every week.
- Context crosses at least two business systems.
- A person currently carries or reconstructs that context.
- Completed examples and important exceptions exist.
- One owner can define the do / ask / never boundary.
- Completion can be verified from a source system or receipt.
- The credible value recovered or protected exceeds $1,000 per month.

If recurrence, authority, or proof is missing, do not force the Pilot. Recommend a prompt,
built-in feature, ordinary automation, existing product, or the $500 Map.

## First-month conversion model

This is a planning model, not a promise:

- 20 approved warm contacts and 5 introduction requests.
- 10–30 researched target accounts, introduced in small batches.
- 10 adjacent operator partners.
- 5 total process walkthroughs.
- 2 paid Maps or clear pilot proposals.
- 1 paid Pilot.

The primary diagnostic is walkthroughs, not impressions. If visits rise while walkthroughs
remain at zero, the call to action or audience is wrong. If walkthroughs happen but no
opportunity qualifies, the signal and ICP are wrong. If qualified opportunities do not
close, inspect value, trust, scope, and payment terms in that order.

## Process walkthrough agenda

1. “Show me the last real item.”
2. Follow it from trigger to verified completion.
3. Ask what happens when information is missing, two systems disagree, or a tool fails.
4. Quantify frequency, minutes, rework, delays, lost revenue, and approval load.
5. Classify the opportunity aloud.
6. Recommend the smallest sufficient next step.

Do not demo for twenty minutes before learning the process. Use the contract-to-kickoff
demo only to clarify a property the buyer's example already exposed.

## Close paths

### Recommend the $500 Map when

- several opportunities compete;
- value or data readiness is still uncertain;
- the buyer needs a 90-day sequence;
- an acceptance test or fixed build quote cannot yet be written responsibly.

The Map fee is paid up front and credited in full to a Pilot.

### Recommend the $3,500 Pilot when

- one recurring job is already clear;
- the intake, two systems, authority boundary, and completion evidence can be named;
- the buyer accepts the three-week scope and founding rate;
- an implementation owner can grant access and supervise the shadow run.

Payment is 50% to start and 50% at handover. A verbal yes is not a closed contract until
the scope is signed and the starting payment is received.

## Follow-up discipline

- Warm and partner outreach: one follow-up after at least five days.
- Researched outbound: proof follow-up, then a respectful close-loop note.
- Walkthrough: same-day written recap with classification and next step.
- Proposal: confirm a decision date and follow up against that date, not an arbitrary
  automated cadence.
- Stop immediately on a decline or opt-out.
