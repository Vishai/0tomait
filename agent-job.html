    WHY THIS IS AN AGENT JOB

    The work survives beyond a conversation.

    1. Trigger

    A signed contract and won deal create a durable work item.

    2. Reconcile

    The job joins five systems and notices what is missing.

    3. Act

    It creates bounded delivery artifacts through tools.

    4. Hold + resume

    Financial and external actions wait for owner approval.

    5. Verify

    Callbacks and receipts—not confident prose—close the case.

    What a free AI conversation can do

    Explain an onboarding checklist, draft an invoice email, or suggest a Zap—after a person collects the context and remembers to ask.

    What the installed job owns

    • Watches for new work without a prompt
    • Maintains one case across multiple systems and time
    • Uses tools within written authority
    • Stops and resumes at approval boundaries
    • Verifies the business result before closing
    AUTHORITY, NOT JUST INTELLIGENCE

    The important design decision is what the job may do.

    May do

    • Read approved client records
    • Link matching records
    • Create a project shell
    • Build the onboarding checklist
    • Draft the deposit invoice

    Ask first

    • Send a financial request
    • Invite external attendees
    • Change scope, price, or terms

    Never do

    • Invent a client or payment
    • Alter contract value
    • Close without source evidence
