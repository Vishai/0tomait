THE AUTOMATION SURVIVAL AUDIT

Does your automation survive its engineer?

Ten yes-or-no questions about the systems your business already runs: the automations, the integrations, the spreadsheet only one person understands. Answer honestly and get your score on the spot. Five minutes.

note: each question maps to a field in the deployment manifest spec, the open standard this practice runs on. The audit is the method, worn as a checklist.

q/01
If the person who built your automation left tomorrow, would everything keep working a month later? manifest: handoff
q/02
Do you know where every credential and API key lives, without asking anyone? manifest: systems[].access.secret_location
q/03
When your automation fails silently, does someone on your team find out before a customer does? manifest: flows[].failure_mode.who_notices
q/04
Can more than one person handle the weird cases your automation hands back to a human? pattern: human-review-queue · backup-operator
q/05
Could you explain why the system was built this way, including what was tried and rejected? manifest: decisions[].rejected
q/06
Are the gaps in your automation deliberate, and written down as deliberate? manifest: flows[].known_exceptions
q/07
Has anyone verified in the last 90 days that your documentation still matches reality? manifest: last_verified
q/08
If a key operator left for two weeks, is there a trained backup who has actually done the job? manifest: lineage + walkthroughs
q/09
Do you know which credentials, contracts, or certificates expire in the next six months? manifest: access.expires · risks.trigger_date
q/10
Could a competent stranger, or an AI agent, maintain your system from what is written down today? tools: brief.js · the briefing test
