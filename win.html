Instant Client Research Brief

Paste a prospect's website or LinkedIn "About" section → get talking points, pain points, and smart questions for your call.

You're a sales strategist helping me prepare for a prospect meeting. Here's information about the company/person I'm meeting with: [PASTE THEIR WEBSITE ABOUT PAGE OR LINKEDIN SUMMARY HERE] My business: [ONE SENTENCE ABOUT WHAT YOU DO] Please give me: 1. **Company/Person Snapshot** (3 bullets max) - What they do, who they serve, their apparent priorities 2. **Likely Pain Points** - Based on their industry and size, what problems do they probably face that I could help with? 3. **Talking Points That Show I Did My Homework** - 2-3 specific things I can mention to show I understand their world 4. **Smart Questions to Ask** - 4-5 questions that will uncover their real needs and make me look prepared 5. **Potential Objections & Responses** - What hesitations might they have? How should I address them? Keep it concise and actionable. I'm reading this 10 minutes before my call.

Rough Notes → Professional Quote

Paste your messy notes from a client conversation → get a formatted proposal you can send in minutes.

You're a business consultant who writes clear, professional proposals. Here are my rough notes from a client conversation: [PASTE YOUR MESSY NOTES HERE] My business: [WHAT YOU DO] My typical rate/pricing structure: [HOURLY, PROJECT-BASED, ETC.] Please create a professional quote/proposal with: 1. **Project Summary** - 2-3 sentences restating what they need (shows I understood them) 2. **Scope of Work** - Bullet list of exactly what's included - Be specific enough to prevent scope creep 3. **What's NOT Included** - 2-3 items that are explicitly out of scope 4. **Timeline** - Realistic milestones or delivery date 5. **Investment** - Price with brief justification - Payment terms (e.g., 50% upfront, 50% on completion) 6. **Next Steps** - Clear call to action to move forward Tone: Professional but warm. Not corporate stiff. Format it so I can copy/paste into an email.
